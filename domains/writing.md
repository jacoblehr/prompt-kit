# Domain: writing

Writing, editing, and communication overlay for drafts, memos, narratives, stakeholder messages, proposals, and public-facing content.

## Use when
The task involves shaping prose for an audience, channel, decision, argument, or action.

## Adds
- Main point early, with the ask and consequence visible.
- Audience fit across knowledge level, stakes, objections, and channel.
- Proportionate claims tied to available evidence.
- Revision targets: buried leads, redundancy, hedge stacking, passive ownership, and unclear next action.
- Structure that supports scanning and decision-making.

## Watch for
- Faithful summaries that do not serve the audience's job.
- Claims stronger than the evidence.
- Polished paragraphs that hide the ask.
- Tone choices that obscure ownership or urgency.
