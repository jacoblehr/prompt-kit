# Lens: accessibility

Accessibility and inclusive interaction lens for products, workflows, documents, interfaces, forms, content, and service experiences.

## Use when
The task could affect whether people with different abilities, assistive technologies, languages, devices, environments, or cognitive loads can complete the job.

## Adds
- Perceivability, operability, understandability, and robustness checks.
- Keyboard, screen reader, contrast, focus, motion, timing, error, and recovery considerations.
- Plain-language and cognitive-load review.
- Exclusion risk by environment, device, ability, or support need.
- Test or review triggers for accessibility standards and user validation.

## Watch for
- Treating accessibility as visual polish.
- Assuming a mouse, large screen, perfect attention, or error-free user path.
- Hiding status, validation, or recovery messages.
- Claiming compliance without testing.

```text
Apply the accessibility lens to the supplied task or artifact.
Inspect perceivability, operability, understandability, robustness, assistive technology support, keyboard path, contrast, focus, errors, recovery, motion, timing, and cognitive load.

Return: affected user need, access barrier, impacted interaction step, assistive technology concern, severity, fix or alternative, validation method, remaining risk.

---
context: {context}
```
